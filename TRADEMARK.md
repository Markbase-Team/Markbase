# Markbase trademark policy

The Apache-2.0 licence covers the **code**. It does not grant rights to the
**name** or the logo — §6 says so explicitly. This page says what we do allow,
so you do not have to ask.

## Yes, without asking

- Saying your project works with, is built on, or integrates Markbase:
  "Acme Sync **for Markbase**", "a Markbase plugin".
- Using the name in truthful prose, docs, talks, comparisons or articles.
- Unmodified builds distributed under the name, with the licence intact.

## Please don't

- **Name a fork "Markbase"**, or anything close enough to be mistaken for it.
  Fork freely — the licence says you may — but give it your own name.
- Imply we endorse, sponsor or maintain your project when we do not.
- Use the logo as your own, or as part of your own mark.
- Register "Markbase", or a confusable variant, as a trademark or domain that
  suggests it is the official one.

## The rule behind the rules

Someone reading your project's name should not end up believing they are
getting the official thing when they are not. If your use passes that, it is
almost certainly fine.

Unsure? Ask — we would rather answer than litigate.

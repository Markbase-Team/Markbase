# Invite your team

## The admin account

A fresh instance has no accounts, so nobody can invite anyone yet. Open the
instance right after it starts and you get **Set up <your instance>**: an
email and a password, no invite needed. The account created there is the
instance's admin. Do it straight away; whoever reaches an empty instance first
becomes its admin. Then turn public sign-up off in the Supabase project
(Authentication → Sign In / Up).

## Inviting

Anyone signed in can invite, under **Settings → Invite your team**. Nothing is
mailed; the link travels however your team talks.

1. **Add** the teammate's address. It goes on the instance's invite list,
   which shows who has not joined yet; the **×** withdraws an invite.
2. **Send them the join link** shown below it. It creates an account only for
   an address on the list. **New link** retires every link sent so far.

## Joining

The teammate opens the link and presses **Open in desktop app**. The app
restarts on your instance and shows its sign-up in its window, where they
create their account with the invited address. Without the app, the link at
the bottom of the page creates the account in the browser.

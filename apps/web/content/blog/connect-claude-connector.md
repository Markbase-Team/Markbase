Markbase is in Claude's connectors directory, so connecting it takes about a minute and needs nothing installed. You don't need a Markbase account first: you can make one in the middle of connecting. The video shows the whole thing, from finding Markbase in Claude to sharing the first document Claude wrote.

## Connect it

You can do this on claude.ai or in the Claude desktop app. Once it's connected, Markbase also works in Claude on your phone, because they all share one list of connectors.

1. In Claude, open [Markbase in the connectors directory](https://claude.ai/directory/markbase), or search for Markbase among the connectors, and press **Connect**.
2. Sign in to Markbase. New to Markbase? Choose **Sign up** on the same page, and you come straight back to connecting when your account is made.
3. Choose how much Claude may do to your documents: view, comment, edit, or own them, which includes sharing and deleting. Press **Connect**.
4. Back in Claude, ask `How do I get started?`

## What to try first

- **Get started.** Claude shows a card in the chat with your vault, a few things to try, and your newest document. Press one of them, or ask in your own words.
- **Summarize the chat into a document.** After a long conversation, ask Claude to write it up as a Markbase document: a title, the decisions, the open questions. It lands in your vault, and the card shows what Claude wrote.
- **Get comments, not edits.** Ask Claude to review a document and comment on it. It leaves its suggestions as comments on the lines they're about, and your text stays as you wrote it.
- **Share it.** Ask Claude for a share link, so a teammate, or their agent, can read the document, comment on it or edit it. Sharing needs the **Owner** level from step 3; with less, use the **Share** button in Markbase.

## You stay in charge

The level you chose is the most Claude can ever do, whatever it's asked. Claude gets an API key of its own, listed under **API keys** in Markbase, where you can lower its access or revoke it without touching your other agents. Everything Claude changes goes into the document's history under its own name, so you can see what it did and undo it.
